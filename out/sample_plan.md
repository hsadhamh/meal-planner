# Ore Samayal meal plan

**Diet:** non-vegetarian (egg, chicken, mutton, fish, prawn) · **Adults:** 4 · **Children:** 6 · **Baby (months):** 8

Adult protein 82-109 g/day · carbs 33-45% of energy

## Day 1 · Sunday

| Meal | Adults | Children | Baby |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | Samai idli + Vegetable sambar + 2 boiled eggs | Same dishes, less chilli, full grain portion (2 boiled eggs: 1 egg) | Unsalted mini idli from the baby bowl, mashed into plain dal; no salt, sugar or honey |
| Lunch | Red rice (mappillai samba) (1 small katori) + Chicken kuzhambu + Beans-carrot poriyal (double portion) + Cucumber-carrot-onion salad + Curd (1 katori) | Same dishes, less chilli, full grain portion (Red rice: 1 full katori; Chicken kuzhambu: Half the adult protein, lifted before extra chilli) | Chicken boiled separately with turmeric, shredded fine; no salt, sugar or honey |
| 4:30 snack | Black chana sundal (1 katori) | Same dishes, less chilli, full grain portion (Black chana sundal: ½ katori) | Whole legumes and nuts are a choking risk; give mashed fruit with curd; no salt, sugar or honey |
| Dinner | 1 chapathi (only if hungry) + Pepper chicken dry (from lunch) + Cucumber-carrot-onion salad | Same dishes, less chilli, full grain portion (1 chapathi: 2 chapathi; Pepper chicken dry: Milder portion with 2 chapathi) | Lifted before chilli and salt, mashed; or sprouted ragi porridge with ghee; no salt, sugar or honey |

Adult day: 1890 kcal, protein 109 g, carbs 39%, fibre 41 g. Fruit: Apple. Drink: Tender coconut water.
Tonight: Set curd for tomorrow

## Safety notes

- Estimates use approximate food-composition values; real meals vary by 10-15%.
- Baby: breast milk or formula stays the main food; 2-3 meals a day at 6-8 months, 3-4 at 9-11 months (WHO).
- Baby: no honey, salt, sugar, juice, tea or cow's milk as a drink before 12 months (NHS, CDC).
- Baby: grind nuts and seeds; mash legumes; remove every fish bone; skip sardines and prawns.
- Children keep full grain portions; carb reduction applies to adults only.
